# Runtime Recovery Contract

## Configuration

The feature adds strictly validated default-off strategy objects under `detector`. Omitted objects do not enter the effective identity of legacy configurations. Enabling requires `single_real_groove`, v2/v3 physical refinement, original source consistency, ambiguity resolution and 026 edge-family selection.

Numeric recognition, refinement, source and circle gates remain owned by their existing configurations. Recovery objects may only bound candidate/hypothesis capacity and select a strategy version; they must not replace global thresholds.

## Decision contract

1. Preserve raw/original decision objects byte-for-byte in diagnostics.
2. A provisional recognition candidate is never equivalent to accepted until downstream proof completes.
3. Each radial row is sampled once; retained wall peaks and global hypotheses are bounded.
4. Exactly one qualified family per wall and exactly one effective groove candidate are required.
5. Source override is permitted only for exact contrast-only rejection and all original non-contrast checks passing.
6. Mixed/occluded classification requires structural or overlap evidence; contrast asymmetry alone is insufficient.
7. Missing, nonfinite, overflow, zero-family and multiple-family states fail explicitly.
8. Global polar/circle failures remain terminal regardless of local recovery.

## Result safety

On every invalid or ambiguous result, `currentAngleDeg`, all correction fields, direction, mechanical correction and `plcCommand` are null; `plcExecutionAuthoritative` is false. The feature does not change PLC/HMI interfaces or authorize production execution.

## Diagnostic contract

Additive nested diagnostics record schema/strategy versions, enabled status, original/effective decisions, candidate counts, bounded family summaries, selected IDs, passed/failed checks, numeric margins, terminal stage and timing. Runtime diagnostics exclude filenames as evidence, manual truth, private paths and unbounded per-sample arrays.
